I replaced all the parsing logic with some pointer arithmetic that figures out where the semicolon and decimal point are by deduction based on the the fact that there are only 4 cases:
9.9
-9.9
99.9
-99.9

So the ; is always at either n-4, n-5, or n-6. And then you check the first byte to see if it's a minus sign and handle the remaining digits for either the length 3 or length 4 cases.

This is way faster the whole thing is now:
